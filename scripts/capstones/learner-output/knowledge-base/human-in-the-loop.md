# Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)

**Category:** operational  ·  **Importance:** 8/10
**Introduced in:** Lesson 1.1, Lesson 1.3

## Definition
A design pattern where certain agent actions or decisions require explicit human approval before execution. In the HSBC scenario: no automated resolution of breaks without human sign-off. Represents a hard constraint at the system boundary, not merely a preference. HITL is a dial position — the agent can do investigation/recommendation (dial 3) while resolution remains human (dial 0). Reduces risk of compounding errors and maintains regulatory compliance.

## Notes
First introduced in HSBC scenario. Connects to Module 10 (safety/audit) and Module 11 (business cases). Important that HITL can be selectively applied to the highest-risk actions.
