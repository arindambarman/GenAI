# Helix Research Scenario

**Category:** business  ·  **Importance:** 7/10
**Introduced in:** Lesson 1.1

## Definition
A recurring fictional case study: a 40-person biotech startup where researchers spend 30% of time on literature synthesis across 8M PubMed papers. Hard constraint: cite-faithfulness (every claim must trace to a specific paper; hallucinations can derail $4M experiments). Emphasizes memory, retrieval, grounding, and long-horizon reasoning.

## Notes
One of three "World Bible" organizations. Directly motivates RAG (Module 5) and agentic RAG for multi-hop reasoning.

## Related concepts
- uses → `four-agent-properties` (The Helix scenario particularly stresses the memory and reasoning properties — cite-faithfulness requires deep integration of retrieval and grounding into the agent's core loop.)
