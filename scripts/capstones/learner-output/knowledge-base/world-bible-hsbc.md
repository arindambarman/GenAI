# HSBC Mid-Office Reconciliation Scenario

**Category:** business  ·  **Importance:** 7/10
**Introduced in:** Lesson 1.1

## Definition
A recurring fictional case study: a bank's mid-office reconciles ~14,000 SWIFT settlement messages nightly, with ~1,400 breaks requiring investigation. Represents regulated, high-stakes, explainability-mandatory agent deployments. Cost: $3.4M/year in break-handling. Hard constraints: SR 11-7 model risk, GDPR, 7-year audit trail.

## Notes
One of three "World Bible" organizations used throughout the course. Emphasizes evaluation, explainability, governance, and cost discipline.
