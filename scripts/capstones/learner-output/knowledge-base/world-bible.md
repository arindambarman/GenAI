# World Bible (Recurring Business Scenarios)

**Category:** foundational  ·  **Importance:** 6/10
**Introduced in:** Lesson 1.1

## Definition
Three fictional but realistic organizations used as running case studies throughout the course: (1) HSBC Mid-Office Reconciliation — banking, regulated, high-stakes; (2) Helix Research — biomedical startup, cite-faithfulness critical; (3) Acme E-commerce — (truncated in module text). These scenarios provide concrete grounding for abstract concepts and force students to reason about evaluation, compliance, latency, and cost in realistic contexts.

## Notes
Pedagogical scaffold; understanding these scenarios is required to get full value from business-scenario sections in later modules.
