# Recommended Learning Paths

## 1. Practitioner Path: Building Your First Agent
**Audience:** Software engineers, product managers, AI/ML practitioners new to agents  ·  **Time:** ~4.5 hours

Fast-track path focused on practical decisions and implementation. Emphasizes the Agency Dial, decision framework, and paradigm selection. Skip historical context; focus on ReAct and failure-mode recognition.

**Lessons in order:**
1. Lesson 1.1
1. Lesson 1.3
1. Lesson 1.4
1. Lesson 1.2

## 2. Researcher Path: Understanding the Foundations
**Audience:** Researchers, academics, those building novel agent architectures  ·  **Time:** ~6 hours

Comprehensive path tracing the intellectual lineage from symbolic agents through RL to modern LLMs. Builds intuition on what concepts are timeless (planning, exploration) and what changed (representation). Prepares for Module 2 mathematical foundations.

**Lessons in order:**
1. Lesson 1.2
1. Lesson 1.1
1. Lesson 1.4
1. Lesson 1.3

## 3. Business/Product Path: Evaluating Agent Investment
**Audience:** Business leaders, product strategists, stakeholders deciding whether to build agent capabilities  ·  **Time:** ~3 hours

Focuses on the decision framework and cost-of-agency concepts. Emphasizes when NOT to build agents and understanding failure modes before committing resources. Light on paradigms; heavy on decision criteria.

**Lessons in order:**
1. Lesson 1.1
1. Lesson 1.3
1. Lesson 1.4
